Network policy, enforced at the switch.

The system at a glance

One control plane.
One enforcement point.

Ryu decides what the firewall policy should be. OVS executes that decision on packets. The REST response, switch table, and ping results are checked independently.

Administrator requests a block

POST /firewall/block/10.0.0.2 reaches Ryu on loopback port 8080.

02

Ryu programs s1

Two priority-100 IPv4 drops match h2 as source or destination; a barrier confirms switch processing.

03

The network proves it

ovs-ofctl sees both drops. Pings involving h2 fail; h1 and h3 continue to communicate.

Observed behavior

A reversible change in three states.

The network was measured before the block, during it, and after exact deletion of the drop rules.
